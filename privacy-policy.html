Privacy Policy for NibLog

Last updated: February 6, 2026

Overview

NibLog ("the App") is a fountain pen ink tracking application developed by Sebastian Doyle. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

NibLog does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data you enter into the app (pen details, ink samples, settings) is stored locally on your device using Apple's built-in storage mechanisms.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to any information you enter into the app. If you delete the app, all locally stored data will be removed.

Third-Party Services

NibLog uses Apple's StoreKit framework for in-app purchases and subscriptions. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect usage data, crash reports, or any other telemetry from the app.

Children's Privacy

NibLog does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
